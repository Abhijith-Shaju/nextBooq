# Shipping and Delivery Policy

**Last Updated: 8 September 2026**

nextBooq is a table reservation service. **Nothing is physically shipped.**
There is no courier, no tracking number, and no delivery address, because
there is no parcel — what you receive is a reserved table at a café, held for
you at the time you chose.

This page exists because payment providers and the Consumer Protection
(E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 expect a delivery policy to be published, and
answering "not applicable" without explanation is less useful than saying what
actually happens.

---

## 1. What you are paying for

When you book through nextBooq you pay a **reservation fee** to the café. In
return, that café holds a table for your party at the date and time you
selected.

The fee is collected by the café through its own payment account. nextBooq
never holds your money — see the [Cancellation and Refund
Policy](https://www.nextbooq.in/legal/refunds).

---

## 2. When your booking is delivered

**Immediately.** Delivery is electronic and happens the moment your payment is
confirmed:

| Step | When |
|---|---|
| Payment confirmed | Instantly, at checkout |
| Reservation confirmed and the table held | Instantly, in the same step |
| Confirmation email sent | Within a few minutes |
| Booking visible in **My Bookings** | Instantly |

If your payment succeeds but you do not see the booking, contact us — see §5.
Do not book a second time; you may be charged twice.

---

## 3. The service itself

The table is provided by the **café**, not by nextBooq, at the address shown
on that café's page at the time you booked. Arrive at your reserved time.

Each café sets its own grace period for late arrival, and may release the
table after it. That period is shown on the café's page before you pay.

What happens at the café — the food, the service, the premises — is the
café's responsibility. nextBooq provides the booking, not the meal.

---

## 4. If something goes wrong

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| You paid but no booking appeared | Contact us with your payment reference. Do not pay again |
| The café could not seat you | You are entitled to a refund of the reservation fee — see the [Cancellation and Refund Policy](https://www.nextbooq.in/legal/refunds) |
| You need to cancel | Contact the café or us before their stated cut-off |
| You were charged twice | Contact us with both payment references and we will arrange the duplicate to be refunded in full |

Refunds are issued by the café from its own payment account and typically
reach you within 5–7 business days.
