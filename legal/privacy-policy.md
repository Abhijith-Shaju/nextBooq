## 1. Who is responsible for your data

nextBooq is the **Data Fiduciary** for the account you hold with us and for the operation of the Platform.

Each café you book with ("**Café Partner**") is a **separate Data Fiduciary** for the reservation information it receives about you and for anything it records about you in its own dashboard. When you book a table, your reservation details are shared with that Café Partner so it can honour the booking. Questions about how a particular café uses your information should be directed to that café; we will help you reach them.

## 5. Where your data is stored, and cross-border transfers

Your personal data is stored and processed in **India**. Our application database (MongoDB Atlas) and our API servers (Amazon Web Services) are both located in the Mumbai region (`ap-south-1`).

We do not transfer your personal data outside India for storage. The DPDP Act permits such transfers except to countries the Central Government restricts by notification; if we ever begin storing or processing personal data outside India, we will update this policy before doing so.

Our website is served from a global content delivery network, so static page assets — and the routing check that decides whether you are signed in — may be handled at edge locations outside India. That check reads only whether your session cookie is present and unexpired; it does not read your profile, your bookings, or any other personal data, and it stores nothing.

All storage, and all processing of your account and reservation data, happens in India.

## 7. Your rights as a Data Principal

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

1. **Access** — obtain a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is processed, including the identities of those we have shared it with. This includes the visit and no-show counts a Café Partner holds about you.
2. **Correction and completion** — have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected. You can change your own name, email address and phone number from your profile at any time; anything else, ask us.
3. **Erasure** — have your data deleted once the purpose is served, subject to retention we are legally required to observe (see §6).
4. **Nomination** — nominate another individual to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated.
5. **Grievance redressal** — complain to us first (see §9), and escalate to the Data Protection Board of India if unsatisfied.
6. **Withdraw consent** — at any time, and as easily as you gave it. Withdrawal does not undo processing already carried out, and may mean we cannot honour existing reservations.

**How to exercise these rights.** Access, erasure and nomination are handled by our Grievance Officer rather than through a self-service screen: email **shashanksingh9048@gmail.com** from the address registered on your account and we will respond within **30 days**. Correcting your own name, email or phone does not need a request — do it from your profile.

You are responsible under the DPDP Act for providing accurate information and for not filing false or frivolous complaints.

## 8. How we protect your data

- Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and are never stored or transmitted in readable form.
- Sessions use an `HttpOnly` cookie, so page scripts cannot read your session token.
- Each Café Partner's payment credentials are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM.
- Access to café data is restricted by role, and staff can only see data for cafés they belong to.
- All traffic is served over HTTPS.
- Failed logins, rate-limit blocks, and settings changes are recorded in an audit log.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected users as required by the DPDP Act.
