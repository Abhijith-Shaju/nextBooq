# Cancellation and Refund Policy

**Last Updated: {{TODO:EFFECTIVE_DATE}}**

This Policy explains what the reservation deposit is, when it is refunded, and how to cancel a booking made through nextBooq. It forms part of our [Terms and Conditions](https://www.nextbooq.in/legal/terms) and is published in compliance with the **Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020**.

---

## 1. What you are paying for

When you reserve a table you pay a **reservation deposit**, not the price of a meal.

- The deposit amount is set by each café and is **shown to you in full, inclusive of all applicable taxes, before you pay**. There are no additional charges added at any later stage.
- The deposit secures your table for the date, time, and party size shown at checkout.
- **The deposit is adjusted against your bill at the café.** It is not an extra fee — if you turn up and dine, you effectively pay it towards your meal.
- **nextBooq is not the seller of food, beverages, or dining services.** The café is. nextBooq provides the booking technology.

**Where your money goes:** each café collects deposits into **its own Razorpay account**. Your deposit is paid to the café directly and is never held by nextBooq. This is why refunds are issued by the café — see §4.

---

## 2. Holding a table

When you select a time slot, the table is held for you for a short window (shown at checkout — typically **10 minutes**) while you complete payment.

- If payment is not completed within that window, **the hold expires automatically**, the table is released back to availability, and nothing is charged.
- An expired hold is not a booking, and no deposit is due.

---

## 3. Cancelling a reservation

> **Please note:** nextBooq does not currently offer a self-service cancellation button in the booking flow. Cancellation is handled manually, as described below. We intend to add self-service cancellation to the Platform.

To cancel a confirmed reservation:

1. **Contact the café directly**, using the phone number shown on its booking page and in your confirmation; **or**
2. **Email nextBooq support at {{TODO:SUPPORT_EMAIL}}**, quoting your reservation reference.

Do this **before the café's stated cancellation cut-off**, which is shown to you at the time of booking. The café then cancels the reservation from its dashboard, which releases the table and triggers a refund.

---

## 4. When you get your deposit back

| Situation | Outcome |
|---|---|
| **You cancel before the café's cut-off** | **Full refund** of the deposit to your original payment method |
| **You attend your reservation** | Deposit is **adjusted against your bill** at the café |
| **The café cancels** (closure, operational issue, overbooking) | **Full refund**, in every case |
| **The Platform fails** — payment succeeded but the booking could not be honoured | **Full refund.** Such payments are automatically flagged for manual refund review |
| **You do not arrive** (no-show) | The deposit **may be forfeited to the café**, per that café's stated no-show policy and grace period, both shown to you before you pay |
| **You cancel after the café's cut-off** | Treated per that café's stated policy — which may be full forfeiture. Check the policy shown at booking |

### Refund timelines

Refunds are initiated by the café through its Razorpay account. Once initiated, the amount typically reaches your original payment method within **5–7 business days**, depending on your bank or card issuer. nextBooq does not control this timeline.

If a refund you are owed has not arrived within **10 business days**, contact us at {{TODO:SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and we will follow it up with the café on your behalf.

## 5. A note on no-shows

Deposit forfeiture on a no-show exists so that cafés holding a table for you are not left out of pocket when nobody arrives. To be fair about it:

- the grace period and the forfeiture consequence are shown to you **before** you pay;
- if you tell the café you cannot make it before their cut-off, you get a **full refund**;
- if you arrive late but within the grace period, your table is honoured;
- if you believe a deposit was forfeited unfairly, raise it with the café first, and escalate to our Grievance Officer (§7) if it is not resolved.

## 6. Failed and duplicate payments

- If your payment fails, no booking is created and no amount is captured. If your bank shows a pending amount, it is an authorisation hold that is normally released within 5–7 business days.
- If you are charged twice for the same reservation, contact {{TODO:SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with both payment references and we will arrange the duplicate to be refunded in full.
